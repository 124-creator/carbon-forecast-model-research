## Common predictor
Reuse inspected price-prefix, causal 31-member statistical reference, training support and GRU infrastructure from Stage M, not the old trained models. GRU(1,16) takes 60 standardized observed price changes. Let f=tanh(U(h(x)-h(0))+Vq+c)-tanh(c), dimension 8. q is the existing causal reference context [tanh(mu/s_T),rho*(1-v),1-v], not an economic-state truth. Reference b is saved as issued and never revised by later parameter selection. Final forecast is p_t+b[t,h]+s_T*c[t,h], h=1..20.

All spline versions produce six values a=3*tanh(W_a f+b_a) and drift d=tanh(w_d f+b_d), initialized to zero. The initial nodes are the previous FIXED6 nodes round(geomspace(1,20,6)) = [1,2,3,6,11,20], plus node (0,0). Thus interpolation preserves the zero-origin response. The final correction adds (h/20)*d, as in the fixed/rank controls. This drift has a redundancy with linear spline components; we retain it equally instead of attributing a coordinate change to innovation.

## Single candidate: bounded conditional mesh
Keep the first/last future nodes exactly 1 and 20. There are five positive gaps g and four free logits. Let g0=diff([1,2,3,6,11,20]), epsilon=0.25, D=19-5*epsilon=17.75, p0=(g0-epsilon)/D.

For MESH compute z=[2*tanh(A f+b),0], A in R^(4x8), b in R^4, initialized at zero. Then
  g_k=epsilon+D*softmax(log(p0)+z)_k,
  tau=[1,1+cumsum(g)],
  c[t,h]=linear_interpolate([(0,0),(tau_k,a_k)],h)+(h/20)*d.
Interpolation is evaluated differentiably (continuous piecewise affine, almost-everywhere parameter derivatives), not by reading future observations. Node values and locations depend only on issuance-time history. Gaps are at least epsilon; knots cannot cross or collapse. No monotonicity is imposed on price responses. This is a conditional free-knot output family, not a new generic spline theorem, an identified market event clock or a universal risk guarantee.

## Pre-fit checks, diagnostics and stop
Tests before new training: exact equality at zero logits to prior FIXED6; common parameter initialization; minimum gap/endpoints; interpolation at knots; affine reproduction when values correspond to affine functions; bounded interpolation; away-from-knot derivative finite differences; static-limit equivalence; zero-output/reference identity; prefix invariance and mature-target support. A deterministic shifted-kink representability example is allowed as algebra, not synthetic market performance or evidence that carbon actually has moving kinks. Changing mesh is redundant for affine curves and a constant mesh can be represented by a shared basis; both boundaries must be recorded.

Mechanism continuation requires MESH improves primary R at least1% versus each of FIXED6,GLOBAL,TILT and wins>=3/4 paired seeds against FIXED6 and TILT. Accuracy continuation also requires >=1% improvement versus strongest freshly trained noncandidate pool, >=2% versus RW, improvement in same-four-seed ensemble versus that pool, and neither year >5% worse than RW. These are project screening thresholds, not journal acceptance standards. If failure, stop this concrete candidate; do not designate a better control as a new original model or open later prices. If passed, it is only a development lead, not novelty confirmation or a publishable final experiment.

Descriptive paired moving-block bootstrap within each year, lengths20/40/60,2000 draws, identical draws for all models/horizons. Repeated development and selection prohibit confirmatory claims.
