## Full predictor and exact support fit
Encoder: the same GRU(1,16) and reference-conditioned8-dimensional features f=tanh(U(h(x)-h(0))+Vq+c)-tanh(c). Let z=[f,1] in R9. R_i=(p[i+1:i+21]-p[i]-b_i)/s. For support A, lambda=.01 fixed:
 D_A = (2 Z_A'Z_A/nA + lambda I)^(-1) Z_A'R_A/nA.
It is the unique minimizer of (||R_A-Z_A D||_F^2+||Z_A D||_F^2)/nA + lambda||D||_F^2. The correction-square coefficient1 is inherited from exposed earlier development and is not an independently discovered optimum. It shrinks an unrestricted population correction toward half the reference residual; this algebra is not automatically the cause of prior failures.

For each A->B pair, U_B=Z_B D_A and
 S_AB=(R_B'U_B+U_B'R_B-2U_B'U_B)/nB-lambda D_A'D_A.
Let S be the equal average of the two symmetric S_AB. Candidate P is the projector onto the at most6 eigenvectors with strictly positive eigenvalues (numeric threshold1e-10), prioritizing largest eigenvalues; zero if none. For fixed encoder and D_A, it minimizes the average B criterion ||R_B-U_B P||^2/nB+||U_B P||^2/nB+lambda||D_A P||^2 over orthogonal projectors rank<=6. Proof is trace expansion/Ky Fan, not a new theorem. Minimized encoder objective is the average ||R_B||^2/(20nB) minus the sum of retained eigenvalues/20. Backpropagate through eigenvalues and ridge solutions; do not claim derivative at tied eigenvalues or rank boundary.
After each parameter update and at validation/inference, form P from F's forward pairs, refit D_F on all F and issue delta_hat=b+s*z D_F P. P and D_F are saved with every fitted encoder. Decoder is fixed between40-step model refreshes. No later labels revise earlier predictions. Projection directions describe predictive response geometry, not economic factors or a calibrated regime.

## Matched controls and necessary equivalences
FWD_FIXED: same forward pairs/encoder learning but P fixed to orthogonal projector on the previous six-node interpolation span [1,2,3,6,11,20]. IN_SAMPLE: use A=B=F to form S and train the same spectral rule, which reduces to a standard penalized reduced-rank fitting criterion. VARPRO_FIXED: A=B=F and fixed interpolation P. FROZEN_GAIN: same candidate projection/head at random initial encoder; NO neural optimization, so not counted as neural training. Original FIXED6/RANK6/GRU baselines rebuilt from inspected prior source with this stage's shared scale and the same160/patience20 budget.
Fixed encoder/selected P gives ordinary linear regression in learned features; fixed projection is an output transform of a differentiable ridge learner. GAIN's only tentative increment is selecting and learning a response subspace by purged forward predictive gain, not inventing ridge/meta-learning/eigenvectors. Same-time in-sample S, pure noise, reversed-response and known stable-response examples must be retained; empirical positive eigenvalues do not imply true future gains. Refitting D_F can change the model used to select P, so finite-pair optimality does NOT guarantee full-refit or future risk.
