## Causal working posterior
Use the existing 31-member scalar Gaussian reference family: exact RW plus rho in [0,.25,.5,.75,.9,.97] and permanent variance fraction q in [.02,.1,.25,.5,.8], r=1-q. Filter all members causally, initialized z=0 and P=r/(1-rho^2). For each increment, e=dp-(rho-1)z; S=(rho-1)^2P+1; C=rho(rho-1)P+r; z_new=rho*z+C*e/S; P_new=max(rho^2P+r-C^2/S,0).
At origin60 and every20 observed steps, choose the member with minimum profile innovation loss over past W=240 innovations, excluding the current origin. Estimate the common variance scale vhat=mean(e^2/S) on that same past support; hold both until the next reference refresh. Current reference mean mu=z_current, conditional variance V=vhat*P_current; b_h=(rho^h-1)*mu for h1..20. Archive each as-of value; never rewrite historical reference forecasts. This V is conditional on an estimated working model; it excludes model-selection/parameter uncertainty and is not measured price noise or authenticated latent-state truth.

## Candidate architecture
A 16-state GRU-style encoder processes 60 observed increments x, reference means m=mu/s and variances v=V/s^2. s is the current fit training-subset increment standard deviation. Standard reset and retention gates depend on x and previous deterministic hidden state only. Let eta_i be the standard GRU candidate preactivation excluding the latent input, and a_i an additional learned coefficient. The candidate proposal is
psi_i=exp(-a_i^2*v/2)*sin(eta_i+a_i*m).
Update h_i=z_i*hprev_i+(1-z_i)*psi_i. A zero-initialized linear20 head produces correction; final normalized delta=b/s+head(h_last). No forced removal of main effects. a is projected to [-3,3]. Initial a=.5; h0=0. This integrates one sine proposal over N(m,v) while holding previous hidden state/gates fixed, not the full stochastic recurrent trajectory. Hidden values stay in [-1,1], but there is no global contraction, state identification or risk guarantee.

## Three matched mechanism controls
POINT: identical cell, attenuation=1 (v not used).
FREE_DAMP: attenuation=exp(-d_i^2*v/2), with separate learned d initialized equal to a and bounded [-3,3]. Candidate is exactly its tied-parameter restriction d=a; this equivalence must be tested and disclosed.
CONSTANT: identical candidate but replace v throughout each input window by the mean variance on the current fit training inputs. Tests whether time-varying working uncertainty adds information beyond static damping.
All common parameters initialize identically at each seed/refresh. Controls do not pretend equal effective information or exact FLOPs; variance ablations intentionally remove the mechanism. FREE_DAMP has16 extra parameters.
Additional neural baselines: FULL from Stage I (standard GRU historical encoder plus unconstrained tanh combination with reference state, freshly trained); ordinary GRU(1,16),20 head, freshly trained. Same observed prices and maturation rules. FULL uses existing mean/rho/q reference features, not V; FREE_DAMP is the key equal-variance-information baseline.
