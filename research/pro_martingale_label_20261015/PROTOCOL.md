## Atoms and mean-preserving smoothing
For every fit/horizon, only training labels set A_h=max(2,1.05 max_i |t_ih|). J=17 atoms c_hj=A_h sign(u_j)|u_j|^2, u_j equally spaced on[-1,1]. Atom support is not tuned; all training t lie strictly inside. Future targets may exceed it, and violation counts must be reported without clipping evaluation. This is a bounded categorical mean model, not universal extrapolation.
q0(t) places probability on the two bracketing atoms with barycentric weights, so sum q0=1 and q0*c=t exactly. For interior atom j, deltaL=c_j-c_(j-1),deltaR=c_(j+1)-c_j, define generator
 Q[j,j-1]=1/(deltaL*(deltaL+deltaR)),
 Q[j,j+1]=1/(deltaR*(deltaL+deltaR)),
 Q[j,j]=-sum_offdiag.
Endpoints are absorbing (zero rows). Thus Q*1=0,Q*c=0. Set diffusion time tau_h=(.3 max(RMS_training(t_h),.1))^2. T_h=expm(tau_h Q_h). Unique candidate labels qMP=q0 T. Their mass and first moment are preserved on this finite support; boundaries are absorbing rather than reflection. At tau=0 recover two-hot; near endpoints distribution can be asymmetric. These are label smoothing operators, NOT physical carbon transition probabilities and NOT denoising the observed future path. expm is computed once per fit/horizon, not neural trajectory simulation.

## Neural output surface
Categorical versions share encoder and a6-by17 logit coefficient tensor generated by a linear layer from8 features. Fixed six-node interpolation B on[1,2,3,6,11,20] maps coefficients into20-by17 logits. Add a symmetric training-only prior logit from Gaussian atom-bin masses centered at0 with width max(RMS(t_h),.1). Coefficient layer starts at0; prior symmetry implies initial correction0. Probabilities softmax over17 atoms; correction=sum_j prob*c_hj. Predictive mean is bounded by atom support. Probabilities approximate smoothed shrinkage residual labels, not the calibrated true price density; do not claim CRPS or calibrated intervals without separate work.
