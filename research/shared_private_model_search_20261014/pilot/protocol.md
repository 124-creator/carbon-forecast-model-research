# Frozen synthetic mechanism pilot
Seeds 17,29. No market files. NumPy analytic-backprop tanh MLPs, not Transformer training. Four tasks, first three share nonlinear response with opposite/different private dynamics; fourth has unrelated nonlinear response and is the negative-transfer control. Inputs only five observed exogenous time-series values, no latent/oracle components. Chronological 64 train, 64 validation, 384 test; train-only scaling. 1200 full-batch Adam steps, lr .008, checkpoint every 100, validation-only checkpoint selection. Two fixed seeds, four methods; no tuning after scores. Under 15 minutes.

All methods have exactly 4 banks of 12 tanh units: W[4,5,12], b[4,12], v[4,12], c[4]; 340 trainable scalar parameters. Independent uses its own bank. Fully shared averages all banks (task-specific intercept only). Shared-private pools first six units from all banks and uses own final six units. The orthogonality ablation adds .1 * mean_t Cov_train(shared,private_t)^2 to the same shared-private model. This is a conventional output-covariance decorrelation constraint, not new architecture or identification. Equal aggregate parameter count and optimizer steps, NOT equal per-task accessible capacity, effective rank, or FLOPs. All methods see same observed arrays and labels, independent gradient access restricted to own task. Fully shared cannot learn task-specific nonlinearities by design; this is a mechanism control, not a strongest multi-head baseline.

No innovation candidate is asserted: task-conditioned coefficients, shared/private decomposition, orthogonality and gradient surgery are existing ideas. Relevant primary sources: Liu et al ACL 2017 https://aclanthology.org/P17-1001/ and Yu et al NeurIPS 2020 https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.06782 . PCGrad is not implemented or renamed. This deliverable is the mechanism foundation only. Orthogonality of learned outputs does not establish causal or identifiable components.
